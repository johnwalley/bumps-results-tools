# Bumps Results

Results data for bumps racing (Cambridge Lents and Mays, Oxford Eights and Torpids, Cambridge Town Bumps), processed into per-crew outcomes and per-club statistics.

## Competitions

**Set**:
A bumps competition that recurs year on year, such as May Bumps or Torpids.
_Avoid_: Competition, series

**Event**:
One recorded run of racing within a Set for one gender, corresponding to exactly one results file. A year may contain several Events. This is an internal term, not one used with readers of the results.
_Avoid_: Set, race, year

**Complete**:
Describes an Event whose results are recorded for every day of racing.
_Avoid_: Full set, finished

## Crews and order

**Crew**:
One boat entered by a club, identified by club and number, such as Pembroke II.
_Avoid_: Boat, entry, position

**Position**:
A place in the order of crews, counted from Head. A Crew holds one Position at the start of each day and may hold a different one at the finish.
_Avoid_: Crew, place

**Head**:
The first Position.

## Outcomes

**Headship**:
Finishing Head at the end of a year's racing. If a year has several Events, only the last one decides the Headship.
_Avoid_: Head of the River (as a count)

**Blades**:
The award a Crew earns by bumping up on every day of an Event, or by winning the Headship.
